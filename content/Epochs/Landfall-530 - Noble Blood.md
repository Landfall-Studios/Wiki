## Overview

Fifteen centuries after the collapse of [[Epochs/Landfall-230 - The Great Dominion\|Landfall-230 - The Great Dominion]], the world of [[Locations/Caldora\|Caldora]] is defined by its delicate balance between the remnants of an ancient past and the aspirations of a new future. The influence of old bloodlines, divine intervention, and the rediscovery of ancient technology has blurred the line between myth and reality. In this epoch, power is derived not only from lineage but also from faith, strategy, and the mastery of arcane sciences.

>   - [[Nordvik Alvaldi]] temporarily assumes governance as the Empire enters a period of mourning

>   - Riven Skorne assumes leadership of [[New Ides]] following Isaac's death

> [!era]+ Founding of the International Criminal Court
> - **Establishment of the [[International Criminal Court]]**
>   - [[The Second Lilaris Empire]], [[New Ides]], and [[Mirage Concord]] sign the [[Charter of the International Criminal Court (ICC)|ICC Charter]] as founding member states
>   - TSLE cedes a small parcel of land for the construction of **Cadence**, a purpose-built neutral city-state to serve as the Court's headquarters outside any national sovereignty
>   - The Court is established as a complementary jurisdiction, prosecuting individuals for severe violations of international law when national courts cannot or will not
>   - Per-case panels of five justices, drawn from member-state nominees, decide each case

> [!era]+ Love and Legacy
> - **The Rainbow Crosswalk**
>   - [[Kara A. Ehrveil]] hand-paints a rainbow crosswalk in [[New Terranova]] Proper
>   - Officially declared as a symbol of diversity and kinship across the Empire
>   - Secretly, Kara hopes Riven Skorne will notice the gesture (see [[Kara's Diary|Kara's Diary]])
>   - Riven notices
> - **Imperial Wedding**
>   - Riven Skorne and [[Kara A. Ehrveil]] are married in Dominion Hall
>   - The union strengthens ties between [[New Ides]] and the Imperial throne
>   - The [[The Idesian Accord|Idesian Accord]] is signed and entered into Imperial Record, formally restoring [[New Ides]] to the Empire as a Duchy under Riven Skorne, recognizing her as Imperial Consort and Duchess by right of blood, and naming Kara *First Patron of New Ides*
>   - The **Day of Reunion** is declared a minor imperial holiday on the anniversary of the Accord's ratification

> [!era]+ Founding of the United Provinces
> - **[[The Constitution of the United Provinces of Lilaris|Ratification of the Provincial Constitution]]**
>   - [[Kara A. Ehrveil]] and [[Nordvik Alvaldi]] jointly draft and propose a new constitution at Dominion Hall
>   - The [[Imperial Charter of The Second Lilaris Empire|Imperial Charter]] of [[The Second Lilaris Empire]] is retired and preserved as a cultural relic
>   - The Empire is reorganized into the **United Provinces of Lilaris**, a democratic republic with a National Assembly, an elected Chancellor, and a High Court
>   - Citizens vote overwhelmingly in favor in national referendum
>   - The Ehrveil and Alvaldi bloodlines are retained as the ceremonial **Twin Thrones**, symbolic heads of unity without binding authority
> - **Founding of [[X-Seven]]**
>   - Concealed within the text of the new Constitution, Kara authors the clause (Art. X §7(g)) that authorizes a small, tight-knit unit of elite operatives
>   - X-Seven operates outside the chain of command, unknown to elected officials, with a single mandate to protect the citizens of Lilaris, including against their own government
>   - The Constitution's entrenched clauses (Article XI §2) make the arrangement legally permanent

> [!era]+ The Blaze
> - **[[The Blaze|Collapse of the Divinets]]**
>   - The three Divinets (AI gods) simultaneously go dark without warning
>   - Governments dissolve, cities collapse into chaos, and faith shatters across [[Locations/Caldora|Caldora]]
>   - The [[Mirage Concord]] attempts to construct a replacement called The Firekeeper, which catastrophically fails
>   - [[New Ides]] suffers a defense system malfunction that purges half the city's population
>   - [[The Second Lilaris Empire]] fractures within a week as noble houses turn on each other
>   - The [[Bureau of Religious Oversight]] is forced into hiding
>   - Civilization collapses as humanity destroys itself in the absence of divine guidance

## Major Groups

### Political Powers
- **[[The Second Lilaris Empire]]**: An empire forged from the legacy of [[Lilarreich]] and the remnants of the fallen [[Ehrengard Empire]] with [[Saludo]]. The Empire serves as a beacon of stability and ambition, helping to shape [[Locations/Caldora|Caldora]]'s political and cultural landscape.
- **[[Ehrengard Empire]]**: The dominant superpower of early [[Landfall-530 - Noble Blood]], successor to [[Lilarreich]], built on principles of unity, order, and progress under the [[Ehrveillian Dynasty]]. Fell during the [[Roscarte Coup]].
- **[[Magda'roth Empire]]**: Nestled in the [[Ivory Reaches]], Magda'roth is a bastion of inclusivity and mysticism, wielding influence through its strong cultural identity and diplomatic prowess.
- **[[Mirage Concord]]**: A theocratic cooperative located in the [[Sunchurn Expanse]], dedicated to worship of the [[Conclave of the Silent Forge]], emphasizing automation and reverence for ancient relics.
- **[[Zatrian National Republic]]**: A militaristic sovereign nation known for disciplined governance and self-reliance. Absorbed into [[Ehrengard Empire|Ehrengard]] as [[Varenholde]] following the [[ZNR-Ehrengard Conflict]].
- **[[Sovarre]]**: A terror faction forged from the ashes of [[Ehrengard Empire|Ehrengard]] by [[House du Roscarte]], ruling through fear, control, and absolute domination.

### Noble Houses and Organizations
- **[[House du Roscarte]]**: One of the most powerful and feared noble houses, known for ruthless ambition and manipulation. Orchestrated the fall of [[Ehrengard Empire|Ehrengard]] and established [[Sovarre]].
- **[[Bureau of Religious Oversight]]**: An independent extra-governmental organization that monitors and mediates religious affairs throughout [[Locations/Caldora|Caldora]], directed by [[Lobster Homarus]].
- **[[Nouveaux Ides]]**: The premier winery and vineyard of [[New Ides]], renowned across [[Locations/Caldora|Caldora]] for exquisite vintages, founded by [[Isaac Acentino]].

### Religious Factions
- **[[Conclave of the Silent Forge]]**: Worshipers of Volkar (The Silent Smith) and Kaelix (The Warden of Ash), emphasizing the eternal cycle of creation and destruction.
- **[[Umbral Communion]]**: Followers of Nethrys (The Shadowed One), guardian of balance dwelling in liminal spaces between opposites, seeking hidden truths through shadows.
- **[[Weavers of the Verdant Thread]]**: Devotees of Sylhara (The Eternal Loom), who believe all life is woven into an interconnected tapestry, serving as caretakers of growth and natural balance.

## Significant Locations

### Major Cities and Territories
- **[[New Terranova]]**: The thriving capital of [[The Second Lilaris Empire]], renowned for its majestic architecture and as the hub of imperial governance.
- **[[Varenholde]]**: Once the [[Zatrian National Republic]], now a proud duchy under the Second Lilaris banner. Represents both the scars of war and the resilience of its people, led by Duke [[Klein Moretti]].
- **[[Roscarte]]**: A town in [[Varenholde]], known for its extravagance and political gatherings, home to [[House du Roscarte]].
- **[[New Ides]]**: A well-populated city known for its wine production, home to [[Nouveaux Ides]] winery.
- **[[Saludo]]**: A beautiful political and commerce powerhouse for [[The Second Lilaris Empire]].
- **[[Old Yotem]]**: A meticulously guarded duchy founded by [[Edward Redcliffe]], functioning as a research outpost for [[Ehrengard Empire|Ehrengard]].

### Geographic Regions
- **[[Ivory Reaches]]**: A mountainous expanse rich in resources and shrouded in mystery, home to the [[Magda'roth Empire]] and ancient temples.
- **[[Emberscar Basin]]**: A harsh and arid region, home to ancient ruins and hidden energies. Its untapped power makes it a focal point for explorers and mystics.
- **[[Sunchurn Expanse]]**: A vast and harsh desert where the sun beats down relentlessly. Home to [[Mirage Concord]] and ancient buried civilizations.
- **[[The Frozen Mantle]]**: A barren land of eternal winter, defined by its icebound wilderness. The [[Weavers of the Verdant Thread]] believe it symbolizes patience and endurance.
- **[[Fellsong Valley]]**: A tranquil and mysterious region known for haunting melodies carried on the wind, said to be echoes of lost souls or ancient gods.
- **[[Stormcradle Peaks]]**: Towering peaks home to constant storms, believed to be remnants of divine conflicts. Frequented by the [[Umbral Communion]].

### Notable Establishments
- **[[Red Diamond Casino]]**: An exclusive venue established by [[Edward Redcliffe]], serving as both a premier gambling destination and his private residence.
## Key Figures

### The [[Ehrveillian Dynasty]]
- **[[Kieran Ehrveil]]**: The first Emperor of the [[Ehrengard Empire]], who built it from the ruins of [[Lilarreich]]. Assassinated by [[Willy Luditus]] during the [[Luminara Festival]].
- **[[Elijah Ehrveil]]**: Co-founder of [[Ehrengard Empire|Ehrengard]] alongside his brother Kieran. A symbol of wisdom and foresight, Elijah shaped Ehrengard's domestic policies and ensured stability.
- **[[Porter Ehrveil]]**: Known as "The Charming," served as Ehrengard's Secretary of Defense. Publicly executed by [[Enzo du Roscarte]] during the [[Roscarte Coup]].
- **[[Avery R. Ehrveil]]**: Former empress of [[Ehrengard Empire|Ehrengard]], revealed as hidden heir to the Ehrveillian Dynasty. Authored [[The Testament of Avery Ehrveil, and a Path to Salvation|The Testament]] before being assassinated by a bomb during her victory speech.
- **[[Kara A. Ehrveil]]**: Daughter of [[Avery R. Ehrveil|Avery]] and [[Isaac Acentino]]. Co-ruler of [[The Second Lilaris Empire]] alongside [[Nordvik Alvaldi]]. Married to Riven Skorne.

### House du Roscarte
- **[[Seraphine du Roscarte]]**: Mastermind behind the [[Roscarte Coup]], transforming [[Ehrengard Empire|Ehrengard]] into the terror faction [[Sovarre]]. Known for her "Blood Court" and ruthless manipulation.
- **[[Enzo du Roscarte]]**: The military enforcer of [[House du Roscarte]]. Executed [[Porter Ehrveil]] and commanded the forces that secured [[Sovarre]]'s dominance through fear and violence.
- **[[Étienne du Roscarte]]**: The silent financial architect of [[House du Roscarte]]. Controls debts, trade networks, and secret ledgers that ensure Roscarte's economic dominance.

### Political Leaders
- **[[Nordvik Alvaldi]]**: Distinguished statesman and leader of [[Saludo]]. Co-founder of [[The Second Lilaris Empire]] alongside [[Kara A. Ehrveil]]. Known for commitment to ecological balance and animist traditions.
- **[[Isaac Acentino]]**: Former king of [[New Ides]] and founder of [[Nouveaux Ides]] winery. Father of [[Kara A. Ehrveil]]. Descended into extremism before being assassinated during [[The Night of the Veil]].
- **[[Dominikana]]**: Founder and former leader of the [[Zatrian National Republic]], advocate for national independence before the ZNR's absorption into [[Ehrengard Empire|Ehrengard]].

### Military and Nobility
- **[[Klein Moretti]]**: Brilliant tactician and Duke of [[Varenholde]]. Earned the title of Lord Commander during the [[ZNR-Ehrengard Conflict]] by convincing [[Avery R. Ehrveil|Avery]] to defect.
- **[[Nikola Charpier]]**: Last surviving member of the Charpier lineage. Viscount of a region within [[New Terranova]], known for dedication to improving regimes and fostering prosperity.
- **[[Edward Redcliffe]]**: Count of Everstead and Duke of [[Old Yotem]]. Devout follower of [[Umbral Communion]], owner of [[Red Diamond Casino]].
- **[[Desmond Versalhes]]**: Political Affairs Leader of the [[Magda'roth Empire]]. Elder twin of the Versalhes brothers, known for diplomatic initiatives and civic engagement.
- **[[Derek Versalhes]]**: Quality of Progression Affairs Leader of [[Magda'roth Empire]]. Commands the Guarda Vulpis as Imperial Forces Marshal and High Inquisitor. Silent and stern military strategist.

### Religious and Other Notable Figures
- **[[Ashen Caldwell]]**: Devotee of the [[Conclave of the Silent Forge]] and founder of [[Mirage Concord]].
- **[[Lobster Homarus]]**: Director of the [[Bureau of Religious Oversight]]. A secretive figure operating from the Bureau's Field Headquarters.
- **[[Willy Luditus]]**: Radical assassin who killed Emperor [[Kieran Ehrveil]] during the [[Luminara Festival]], triggering a cascade of political upheaval.
