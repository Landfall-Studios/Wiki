## Overview

Nouveaux Ides is the premier winery and vineyard of [[New Ides\|New Ides]], renowned across [[Locations/Caldora\|Caldora]] for its exquisite vintages, masterful aging process, and deep-rooted winemaking tradition. Founded by [[Isaac Acentino\|Isaac Acentino]], Duke of New Ides, Nouveaux Ides was the first business established in the duchy and quickly became the gold standard for luxury wines and collectible vintages.

## Legacy & Reputation

Nouveaux Ides is best known for its selective aging process, producing some of the most sought-after and prestigious wines in [[Locations/Caldora\|Caldora]]. Each vintage is carefully cultivated in [[The Frozen Mantle\|The Frozen Mantle]]’s unique climate, allowing for a refined and distinct flavor profile. Collectors and connoisseurs prize Nouveaux Ides wines not only for their superior taste but also for their rarity, as many selections are produced in limited quantities and cellared for years before release.

## Business & Expansion

Beyond its winemaking excellence, Nouveaux Ides has played a pivotal role in the economic and cultural expansion of [[New Ides\|New Ides]]. The Nouveaux Ides Manor, a lavish estate and winery, serves as both the center of operations and a gathering place for elite social events, tastings, and diplomatic functions. With the growth of the town surrounding the manor, Nouveaux Ides has become more than just a winery—it is the heart of New Ides' identity.

## Key Figures

- **[[Isaac Acentino\|Isaac Acentino]]**: The founder of Nouveaux Ides.
