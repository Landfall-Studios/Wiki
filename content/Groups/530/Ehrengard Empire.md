#L530
## Overview

The **Ehrengard Empire**, successor to the ideals of [[Lilarreich\|Lilarreich]], was a dominant superpower in [[Locations/Caldora\|Caldora]]. Rooted in the traditions of the [[Ehrveillian Dynasty\|Ehrveillian Dynasty]], the Empire was built on principles of unity, order, and progress, with a centralized governance structure under the Emperor.

After it's fall at the hand of [[House du Roscarte]], Ehrengard and [[Saludo]] combined to form [[The Second Lilaris Empire]].

Ehrengard was known for its sprawling territories, cultural and military dominance, and a sophisticated bureaucracy that balances regional autonomy with Imperial oversight. It stands as both a beacon of stability and a force of ambition, shaping the destiny of Caldora through strategic alliances, military innovations, and a commitment to preserving its cultural heritage.

## Role in Epochs

- [[Epochs/Landfall-530 - Noble Blood\|Landfall-530 - Noble Blood]]: The Empire rose from the ashes of the Great Dominion and the fragmented territories of [[Lilarreich\|Lilarreich]], consolidating power under the leadership of the [[Ehrveillian Dynasty\|Ehrveillian Dynasty]]. This period marked the Empire’s establishment as the central authority in [[Locations/Caldora\|Caldora]].

## Key Figures

- **[[Thomas Ehrveil\|Thomas Ehrveil]]**: The progenitor of the Ehrveillian Dynasty and founding figure of the ideals that would later shape the Empire. His legacy of unity and innovation continues to influence Imperial policies and culture.
- **[[Lucius Ehrveil\|Lucius Ehrveil]]**: The visionary Chancellor of Lilarreich and despite being long passed, a role-model for [[Kieran Ehrveil\|Kieran Ehrveil]] during the construction of Ehrengard Empire.
- **[[Kieran Ehrveil\|Kieran Ehrveil]]**: The founding Emperor for Ehrengard, Kieran oversaw major military advancements and spearheaded the establishment of [[New Terranova\|New Terranova]].
- **[[Elijah Ehrveil\|Elijah Ehrveil]]**: Elijah was a formative member of Ehrengard alongside his brother Kieran. Elijah worked to handle day-to-day operations and ensure the Empire's stability.
- **[[Porter Ehrveil\|Porter Ehrveil]]**: A prominent figure in the Empire’s military-industrial complex, Porter’s contributions to the defense of [[New Terranova\|New Terranova]] solidified Ehrengard’s strategic dominance.
- **[[Avery R. Ehrveil\|Avery R. Ehrveil]]**: Empress of Ehrengard and former Duchess of Varenholde.
- **[[Nikola Charpier\|Nikola Charpier]]**: Serves as count of southern New Terranova, and aids in Ehrengard's industrial might.
- **[[Klein Moretti\|Klein Moretti]]**: Earned the title of Lord Commander in service to Empire during the [[ZNR-Ehrengard Conflict\|ZNR-Ehrengard Conflict]] and Serves as Duke of [[Varenholde\|Varenholde]], having took over after Avery's brief reign.

## Major Events

- **[[ZNR-Ehrengard Conflict\|ZNR-Ehrengard Conflict]]**: A prolonged conflict with Ehrengard culminated in the defection of key military assets and territories to the Empire, notably [[Varenholde\|Varenholde]], under the leadership of [[Avery R. Ehrveil\|Avery R. Ehrveil]] (disguised as Avery Rosendahl).
- **[[Luminara Festival\|Luminara Festival]]**: An annual festival to celebrate accomplishments and to look forward to the year ahead.
- **[[CTC Tower\|CTC Tower]]**: A brief conflict with [[Willy Luditus\|Willy Luditus]] following the assassination of Emperor [[Kieran Ehrveil\|Kieran Ehrveil]].
- **[[Roscarte Coup]]**: The Ehrengard Empire fell to the [[Roscarte Coup]], leading the way for [[Sovarre]].

## Locations

- **[[New Terranova\|New Terranova]]**: The capital city and heart of the Empire, New Terranova is a bustling metropolis renowned for its architectural grandeur, cultural landmarks, and political significance.

## Culture and Legacy

The Ehrengard Empire honors its roots in [[Lilarreich\|Lilarreich]] by preserving its traditions, while also embracing innovation and adaptability. Cultural festivals, architectural achievements, and advancements in science and technology reflect the Empire’s dual commitment to heritage and progress.

The Empire’s military strength, combined with its diplomatic acumen, ensures its position as a stabilizing force in [[Locations/Caldora\|Caldora]]. Its resilience in the face of crises and its ability to unify diverse regions under a single banner solidify its legacy as a model of governance and ambition.
