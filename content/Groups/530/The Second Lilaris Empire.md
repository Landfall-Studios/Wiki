## Overview

The Second Lilaris Empire (commonly **Lilaris**, or **TSLE**) was the dominant power of mid-to-late [[Landfall-530 - Noble Blood]]. It was founded by the merger of the surviving remnants of the [[Ehrengard Empire]] with the failing state of [[Saludo]], in the immediate aftermath of the [[Roscarte Coup]] and the assassination of Empress [[Avery R. Ehrveil]]. The Empire was jointly ruled by Empress [[Kara A. Ehrveil]] of House Ehrveil and Nordvik Alvaldi of House Alvaldi under the Twin Crowns, and governed by the [[Imperial Charter of The Second Lilaris Empire|Imperial Charter]] across an imperial-era that lasted from its founding until the ratification of [[The Constitution of the United Provinces of Lilaris|the Provincial Constitution]].

Late in its history, the Empire underwent a constitutional conversion into the **United Provinces of Lilaris**, a democratic republic in which the Twin Crowns became the ceremonial Twin Thrones and elected institutions assumed governing authority. The conversion was the work of Kara and Nordvik themselves, drafted jointly at Dominion Hall and ratified by overwhelming popular vote.

Lilaris drew its name and legitimacy from the older [[Lilarreich]], the original Ehrengardian state of which it considered itself the rightful continuation. New Terranova remained the capital throughout.

## Founding

### After the Coup

The Empire's founding cannot be understood without [[Roscarte Coup|the Roscarte Coup]]. [[House du Roscarte]]'s seizure of the [[Ehrengard Empire]], the public execution of [[Porter Ehrveil]], the forced abdication of [[Avery R. Ehrveil]], and the conversion of Ehrengard into the terror state of [[Sovarre]] left the entire Ehrveillian-aligned political order in ruins. Avery fled into exile to [[New Ides]], where she eventually authored [[The Testament of Avery Ehrveil, and a Path to Salvation|the Testament]], a public abdication that also called for a new political order to take Ehrengard's place. The resistance retook [[New Terranova]] not long after, and Avery was killed by a bomb during her victory speech.

Her daughter, [[Kara A. Ehrveil]], inherited what remained on the night of her mother's death.

### The Alvaldi Bargain

Kara was a child at the time, and the Ehrveillian house had no functional administration left to draw on. The state of [[Saludo]], meanwhile, had been weakening for years under [[Nordvik Alvaldi]], strained by the loss of [[New Ides]] to Isaac Acentino's independence and the rising influence of foreign cults along its southern border. Kara and Nordvik met in the immediate aftermath of Avery's assassination and agreed to merge their two states into a single new polity. Each retained the symbolic primacy of their own bloodline while pooling resources, territory, and military forces under a unified crown.

The arrangement was framed as a fulfillment of Avery's Testament. In practice it was also a survival measure for both sides.

## Governance

### The Twin Crowns

Sovereign authority in the imperial era was held jointly by the **Twin Crowns**, Empress Kara A. Ehrveil and her co-sovereign Nordvik Alvaldi. Both heads of state held full governing authority. In the early years, with Kara still young, Nordvik handled most of the day-to-day public business while Kara grew into the role. Once Kara reached majority the two governed as equal partners, with Nordvik particularly active in foreign affairs and Kara taking lead on domestic policy and institutional design.

### The Imperial Charter

Domestic governance operated under the [[Imperial Charter of The Second Lilaris Empire|Imperial Charter]]. The Charter established the structure of the Empire, the offices of the Twin Crowns, and the relationships between the Crowns and the noble houses, duchies, and dependent territories. It also provided for the establishment of departments and statutory acts beneath it, including the [[Lilaris Department of Justice Act]], the [[Criminal Code Act (1-LCC)|Criminal Code Act]], and the [[Civil Code Act (2-LCC)|Civil Code Act]], which collectively formed the legal framework of the imperial period.

### Territorial Structure

The Empire was composed of the territories of its two founding states plus duchies and holdings acquired or integrated during the imperial era. Major divisions included [[New Terranova]] as the capital, [[Saludo]] as the second seat and Alvaldi center, [[Varenholde]] as a duchy of formerly Ehrengardian territory under Duke [[Klein Moretti]], and after the [[The Annexation of Whitefall|Annexation of Whitefall]], Whitefall under Viscountess Ashling Goldwound. [[New Ides]] later joined as a duchy through the [[The Idesian Accord|Idesian Accord]].

## Major Events of the Imperial Era

### The Huge Larry Crisis

The most destabilizing internal crisis of the imperial era was the rise of [[Larrism]]. The cult, centered on the figure of Huge Larry, captured the political leadership of [[New Ides]] under King [[Isaac Acentino]] (who was also Kara's father) and the ExoVinian Empire to the south. The crisis touched Lilaris directly when Lilaris Princess Salem Alvaldi was assaulted during a Larry Cult ritual on unclaimed land, an incident the Empire prosecuted as the central charge in [[Case Filing - The People v. Huge Larry|Case No. 593-LIR-NT]] before the New Terranova Imperial Court. The trial collapsed mid-proceeding, and every defendant including Isaac was killed in [[The Night of the Veil]].

### The Annexation of Whitefall

During the same period, the township of Merlot peacefully seceded from the Idesian Empire and was annexed by Lilaris under [[The Annexation of Whitefall|the Annexation Agreement]], renamed Whitefall, and placed under the rule of newly created Viscountess Ashling Goldwound. The Idesian Empire did not recognize the secession. No open military reprisal followed.

### The Idesian Accord

After Isaac's death, leadership of [[New Ides]] passed to Riven Skorne, who worked to distance the duchy from Larrist doctrine and reopened relations with Lilaris. Riven and Kara were married in Dominion Hall. The accompanying [[The Idesian Accord|Idesian Accord]] formally restored New Ides as a Duchy of the Empire under Riven as Imperial Consort and Duchess by right of blood, named Kara *First Patron of New Ides*, and established the **Day of Reunion** as a minor imperial holiday.

### Founding of the International Criminal Court

Lilaris was one of the three founding signatories of the [[International Criminal Court]] (alongside [[New Ides]] and [[Mirage Concord]]). The Empire ceded a small parcel of land for the construction of **Cadence**, a neutral city-state to serve as the Court's headquarters outside any national sovereignty.

## The Constitutional Conversion

Late in their reign, Kara and Nordvik jointly drafted [[The Constitution of the United Provinces of Lilaris]] at Dominion Hall. The document converted the Empire into the **United Provinces of Lilaris**, a democratic republic.

Under the new Constitution:

- The Twin Crowns became the **Twin Thrones**, ceremonial offices held by the Scions of House Ehrveil and House Alvaldi without binding legislative authority.
- Governing authority moved to a **National Assembly** of elected Representatives, an elected **Chancellor** serving as Head of Government, and a **High Court** of up to five Justices.
- The Provinces were established as permanent self-governing entities; subdivisions and cities operated under elected councils.
- Civil rights, including freedom of expression, equality before the law, due process, political participation, and access to public education and emergency medical care, were entrenched against future amendment.

The Imperial Charter was retired upon ratification and preserved as a cultural relic within Dominion Hall. The citizens of the Empire ratified the Constitution by overwhelming popular vote in national referendum.

### X-Seven

Concealed within the Constitution, Kara authored the legal basis for [[X-Seven]], a small, deniable paramilitary unit of elite operatives outside the chain of command. Its mandate is the protection of the Lilarisi people from any threat to their lives or to the continuity of the Republic, including threats originating within the elected government itself. The authorizing clause sits inside an otherwise unremarkable interpretive section ([[The Constitution of the United Provinces of Lilaris|Article X §7(g)]]) and the offices it depends on are entrenched in [[The Constitution of the United Provinces of Lilaris|Article XI §2]] against any future amendment. Elected officials of the Provinces are not informed of X-Seven's existence.

## End

The Empire (in its United Provinces form) was still standing when [[The Blaze]] arrived. The three Divinets fell silent without warning, central institutions collapsed across [[Locations/Caldora|Caldora]], and the noble houses turned on each other within a week. The Republic fractured. What happened to the Twin Thrones, the National Assembly, or X-Seven during the collapse is unknown.

## Role in Epochs

- **[[Landfall-530 - Noble Blood]]**: Established after the Roscarte Coup as the unified successor to [[Ehrengard Empire|Ehrengard]] and [[Saludo]]. Operates as the dominant superpower of the mid-to-late epoch. Converts into the United Provinces of Lilaris through the Provincial Constitution. Fractures during [[The Blaze]].

## Locations

- **[[New Terranova]]**: Capital of the Empire and, later, of the United Provinces. Seat of Dominion Hall, the Royal Palace, and the federal district under the Constitution.
- **[[Saludo]]**: Second seat of the Empire, political and commercial center of the Alvaldi line.
- **[[Varenholde]]**: Duchy of formerly Ehrengardian territory under Duke [[Klein Moretti]].
- **Whitefall**: Township acquired from the Idesian Empire under the Annexation. Imperial holding under Viscountess Ashling Goldwound.
- **[[New Ides]]**: Returned to the Empire as a Duchy through the [[The Idesian Accord|Idesian Accord]] after Isaac Acentino's death.
- **Cadence**: Purpose-built neutral city-state ceded to the [[International Criminal Court]].

## Key Figures

- **[[Kara A. Ehrveil]]**: Empress and co-sovereign. Architect of the Constitution and the founder of [[X-Seven]]. Married to Riven Skorne.
- **[[Nordvik Alvaldi]]**: Co-sovereign and Scion of House Alvaldi. Co-drafter of the Constitution.
- **Riven Skorne**: Duchess of New Ides, Imperial Consort, and Kara's wife. Brought New Ides back into the Empire through the [[The Idesian Accord|Idesian Accord]].
- **[[Klein Moretti]]**: Lord Commander and Duke of Varenholde.
- **Viscountess Ashling Goldwound**: Ruler of Whitefall.

## Foundational Documents

- [[Imperial Charter of The Second Lilaris Empire]]: Foundational governing document of the imperial era. Retired and preserved as a cultural relic upon ratification of the Provincial Constitution.
- [[The Constitution of the United Provinces of Lilaris]]: Successor document. Converted the Empire into the United Provinces and serves as the supreme law of the Republic.
- [[The Idesian Accord]]: The formal restoration of New Ides as a Duchy of the Empire under Riven Skorne, tied to her marriage to Kara.
- [[Annexation of Whitefall]]: The agreement under which Merlot seceded from the Idesian Empire and joined Lilaris as Whitefall.
