---
title: Non-Human Character Guidelines
description: Rules for non-human characters on Landfall SMP. Demi-humans only, science-based origins only, admin approval required.
created: 2025-05-25
---
To maintain consistency within the Landfall SMP canon, the following guidelines define the role of non-human characters, including anthropomorphic (furry) characters, within the server’s lore.

Landfall SMP is a primarily human world. While some non-human traits may exist, **most characters should be human.** Non-human characters must follow strict guidelines to keep the lore consistent. Admins **reserve the right to reject non-human characters** for any reason, including if there are too many already in play.

---

#### **1. Non-Human Characters Must Be Science-Based**

- No full-animal characters (e.g., talking wolves, upright foxes).
- Non-human traits must have a canon scientific explanation—no magic, alchemy, or divine intervention.
- Acceptable origins include:
    - **Genetic modification** (e.g., lab experiments, designer humans, escaped test subjects).
    - **Cybernetic enhancements** (e.g., artificial tails, engineered vocal cords).
    - **Controlled evolution** (e.g., deliberate adaptation projects).
    - **Mutation/Disease** (e.g., skin changes, neural enhancements).

#### **2. Demi-Humans Only**

- Characters can have **minor** animal traits (e.g., cat ears, tails, reptilian skin patches).
- Fully animalistic characters are **not allowed** without a full, approved Lore Catalyst Request (LCR).

#### **3. No Retconning History to Justify a Character**

- A company cannot have done secret experiments unless it has already been established in lore and had the resources and recognition to plausibly conduct said experiment.
- Evolutionary offshoots must have already existed in prior roleplay.
- Any scientific explanation must fit within the established setting, this can vary across timelines.

#### **4. Approval is Required**

- Any non-human character must submit a ticket for review before being canon.
- If a character is not on the wiki, it is considered non-canon reference material until reviewed.

#### **5. Admin Discretion**

- Admins have final say on all non-human characters.
- If there are too many, new non-human characters may be denied outright.
