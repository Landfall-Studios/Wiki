The LCR system allows players—especially Legends with a passion for the canon—to propose major lore events requiring technical assistance or elements beyond the game’s native support (e.g., creating a "Multiversal Extrapolator" to crash together timelines or introducing new mobs for a Sculk Horde invasion).

To submit an LCR, reach out via Direct Message to Divinity or a lore coordinator.

---

## **How It Works**

1. **Proposal Submission**  
    Players submit an LCR detailing:
    - Event concept and narrative goals.
    - Required technical support (e.g., custom items, abilities, or scripted mechanics).
2. **Feasibility Review**  
    Admins review the request for:
    - Enjoyability and engagement.
    - Technical viability within game limitations.
    - Coherence with existing lore.
3. **Canonization & Implementation**  
    Approved LCRs are integrated into the canon as a new timeline, branch, or Prime Epoch, with divine support for implementation.

---

## **Specifics**

1. **Proposals SHALL**
    - Clearly describe the event’s purpose and its intended impact on the lore.
    - Specify technical needs (e.g., custom items, abilities, or scripted mechanics).
2. **Proposals SHOULD**
    - Tie into existing lore or justify introducing new elements.
    - Outline how the event fits into the current timeline or propose a new timeline/epoch.
    - Consider game limitations and propose alternatives where necessary.
3. **Proposals MAY**
    - Include additional lore elements, such as new or NPC factions, themes, or regions.
    - Provide lore-based explanations for technical constraints or changes (e.g., an unstable portal disrupting the timeline).
