The **Divine Entity** represents the impartial, omnipotent force that oversees the Landfall SMP, embodying the admin team’s actions and decisions. When performing administrative tasks, the team steps into the role of the Divine Entity to ensure fairness, resolve conflicts, and maintain the integrity of the server’s canon.

This separation ensures that the administrative team's power as the Divine Entity does not overlap with or influence the abilities, reputation, or actions of their in-game characters. Similarly, the deeds or behavior of a character should never impact how the Divine Entity is perceived.

### **Role of the Divine Entity**

1. **Impartial Oversight**: The Divine Entity enforces server rules, resolves disputes, and protects the canon without bias.
2. **Lore Stewardship**: Major lore events and Divine Decrees, such as a character's forced death or approval of lore-heavy backstories, come from the Divine Entity, not any individual player or character.
3. **Server Integrity**: By embodying the Divine Entity, the admin team ensures a clear distinction between administrative authority and personal gameplay, maintaining a fair and immersive environment for all players.

The Divine Entity exists solely to serve the world of Landfall, ensuring that its story, rules, and community thrive.
