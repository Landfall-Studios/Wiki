This is an overview on the Rules of War, encouraging tactical warfare while preserving the beauty, creativity, and politics of the Landfall SMP.

For large-scale conquest mechanics, see the [[Frontline Mechanic]]. For covert operations, see the [[Sabotage Mechanic]], the [[Imprisonment Rules]], and [[Assassination Rules]].

## **Rules of Engagement**

1. **Target Strategic Objectives**
    - Focus attacks on military targets like bases or supply depots.
    - Avoid unnecessary destruction of civilian or unrelated decorative builds.
2. **Limit Destruction**
    - Mass destruction (e.g., deforestation, lava casting, or city-wide leveling) is prohibited.
    - Tactical weakening is allowed (e.g., breaching walls, destroying turrets).
3. **Minimize Collateral Damage**
    - Use weapons and tactics designed to limit harm to civilian areas and natural landmarks. Avoid scarring the terrain.
4. **Respect Neutral Zones**
    - Do not attack sacred sites, safe zones, or locations designated as neutral by treaty without a clear and compelling narrative reason.
5. **Fair Play**
    - All combat must have narrative or tactical intent. Pure griefing or non-consensual destruction will be rolled back.
    - Repeatedly killing defenseless players or trapping them at their spawn point is forbidden.
6. **Freedom to Enter Contract**
    - These are default rules. Players may negotiate contracts or diplomatic agreements to supersede specific guidelines, such as creating additional neutral zones or altering engagement terms.
7. **Formal Declarations of War**
    - Formal wars must be declared publicly in the designated announcement Discord channel.
    - Make every effort to **ping the leadership** of the opposing faction via Discord.
    - Provide a **brief narrative justification** for the war in the message.
    - Leave an **in-character notice or document** at the capital of the opposing faction (e.g., signpost, book, or message board).

## **Preserving Creativity**

1. **Respect Builders**
    - Excessive destruction of creative or elaborate structures will result in divine rollback.
2. **Encourage Rebuilding**
    - Victorious factions are encouraged to assist with repairs to maintain server balance.

## **Enforcement**

1. **Divine Oversight**
    - The Divine Entity enforces these rules, including issuing rollbacks, combat bans, and resource penalties.
    - All violation reports must include supporting evidence (screenshots, logs, video, or eyewitness testimony).
2. **War Crimes & Punishments**
    - Players or groups who commit excessive destruction, abuse mechanics, or grief repeatedly may face:
        - Resource fines
        - Rollbacks
        - Combat restrictions
        - Forced retreats
3. **Anti-Abuse Measures**
    - Use of proxy groups (e.g., fake towns or corp fronts) to bypass these rules is prohibited and will be corrected via divine reclassification.
