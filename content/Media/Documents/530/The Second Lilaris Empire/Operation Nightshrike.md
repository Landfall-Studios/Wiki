#### **MISSION DIRECTIVE**

**Subject:** Integration of Klein Moretti, a senior Zatrian National Republic (ZNR) official, into Ehrengard’s intelligence network under absolute secrecy.

**Codename:** *Nightshrike*

**Handlers:**

* **Kieran Ehrveil**, Emperor, Ehrengard Empire  
* **Porter Ehrveil**, SECDEF, Ehrengard Empire

**Objective:** Establish Klein Moretti as a covert asset within Ehrengard while maintaining their public persona within the ZNR. Moretti will operate a food business in New Terranova, allowing for discreet intelligence exchanges and maintaining their ZNR cover while feeding disinformation and gathering critical intelligence.

---

#### **OPERATIONAL PHASES**

##### **PHASE I: INITIAL INTEGRATION**

**1\. Silent Recruitment:**

* Klein Moretti has been discreetly recruited into Ehrengard’s intelligence network following extensive evaluation and vetting. Their dissatisfaction with ZNR leadership and willingness to undermine its strategies have been key factors in their selection.

**2\. Establishment of Cover Business in New Terranova:**

* Moretti will establish a bakery and food business on a modest plot in New Terranova. This enterprise will serve as both a public-facing business and a discreet operational hub for intelligence exchanges.  
* The bakery will appear entirely independent, with no visible ties to Ehrengard’s government or intelligence apparatus.

**3\. Secure Logistics:**

* The bakery premises will include hidden communication infrastructure, allowing Moretti to securely transmit intelligence to their handlers, Kieran Ehrveil and Porter Ehrveil.

---

##### **PHASE II: MAINTAINING ZNR TRUST**

**1\. Continued ZNR Engagement:**

* Moretti will maintain regular activities within the ZNR, ensuring their presence remains consistent and trusted. Their visits will be strategically timed to coincide with ZNR leadership observation and key decision-making periods.

**2\. Disinformation Operations:**

* Moretti will subtly introduce false intelligence into ZNR strategic discussions, redirecting their focus toward non-threatening objectives.  
* This information will be carefully crafted to appear credible and beneficial, further reinforcing Moretti’s image as a loyal contributor.

**3\. Public Persona as a Baker:**

* Moretti’s bakery in New Terranova will produce and sell a variety of breads and food items, creating the image of a simple, independent entrepreneur. This persona will deflect suspicion from both ZNR and Ehrengard operatives.

---

##### **PHASE III: INTELLIGENCE OPERATIONS**

**1\. Covert Intelligence Collection:**

* While maintaining their cover, Moretti will leverage their continued access to ZNR leadership to gather actionable intelligence on ZNR strategies, alliances, and vulnerabilities.  
* Intelligence will be securely transmitted to handlers Kieran Ehrveil and Porter Ehrveil, ensuring it remains compartmentalized and untraceable.

**2\. Tactical Manipulation:**

* Through their trusted position, Moretti will subtly influence ZNR decision-making, introducing ideas and strategies that align with Ehrengard’s broader objectives.

**3\. Avoiding Suspicion:**

* Any intelligence gathered by Ehrengard from this operation will be attributed to unrelated sources, ensuring Moretti’s role remains undiscovered.

---

##### **PHASE IV: LONG-TERM POSITIONING**

**1\. Sustained Bakery Operations:**

* Moretti’s bakery in New Terranova will remain active and visible, fostering the appearance of a stable, independent business venture.  
* The bakery will also serve as a meeting point for secure exchanges and directives from Ehrengard handlers.

**2\. Continuous ZNR Influence:**

* Moretti’s ongoing presence within the ZNR will allow for the steady flow of intelligence and disinformation, ensuring long-term destabilization of ZNR’s strategic capabilities.

**3\. Enhanced Strategic Integration:**

* Over time, Moretti may be positioned to influence key ZNR policies and alliances, providing Ehrengard with opportunities to exploit weaknesses or redirect ZNR initiatives.
