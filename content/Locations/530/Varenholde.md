## Overview

Varenholde, formerly the [[Zatrian National Republic]], is a duchy within [[The Second Lilaris Empire]], governed by Duke [[Klein Moretti]]. It was won by the old [[Ehrengard Empire]] in the [[ZNR-Ehrengard Conflict]]. Located in the arid and rugged [[Emberscar Basin]], Varenholde reflects a complex blend of its Zatrian heritage and the ideals of Ehrengard. Though its integration into the old Empire was a contentious process, Varenholde has since flourished as a center for military strategy and resource development. It currently stands as an indispensable asset to Lilaris and continues to be revered for its cultural legacy.
