## Overview

New Terranova is the capital of [[The Second Lilaris Empire]] and the federal seat of the [[The Constitution of the United Provinces of Lilaris|United Provinces of Lilaris]]. It was originally established under the [[Ehrengard Empire]] as the successor city to the historic [[Terranova]] of [[Landfall-230 - The Great Dominion]], inheriting that capital's role as the political and administrative heart of the dominant Lilaris-aligned state. After the founding of the Empire it served as the seat of the [[Ehrveillian Dynasty]] and the working capital of the Imperial government.

Under the Provincial Constitution, New Terranova was designated a federal district belonging to no Province and administered directly by the National Assembly. The city houses [[Dominion Hall]], the royal palace and historic seat of the Twin Thrones; the New Terranova Imperial Court, where [[Case Filing - The People v. Huge Larry|the People v. Huge Larry]] and other major cases were tried; and the working offices of the Imperial departments established by the [[Imperial Charter of The Second Lilaris Empire|Imperial Charter]] and its statutory law.

## Notable Districts and Features

### New Terranova Proper

The historic core of the city, including the boulevard fronting [[Dominion Hall]] and the main civic spaces. The **Rainbow Crosswalk** in New Terranova Proper was hand-painted by Empress [[Kara A. Ehrveil]] and publicly declared a symbol of diversity and kinship across the Empire. Its private meaning, recorded in [[Kara's Diary]], was a signal aimed at [[Riven Skorne]].

### Everstead

A county within New Terranova held by [[Edward Redcliffe]] as Count of Everstead. The northern countryside.

### Dominion Hall

The royal palace at the city's center. See [[Dominion Hall]] for a full account.

## Major Events

### Huge Larry's Provocations

Across the weeks preceding the Princess Salem Incident, [[Huge Larry]] carried out a series of provocations targeting New Terranova: unlawful entries into Dominion Hall, defacement of the Hall's nameplate and the city's welcome sign, and a public declaration claiming personal sovereignty over the city, with state assets renamed in his own name. These actions formed Case No. 572-LIR-NT and were later folded into [[Case Filing - The People v. Huge Larry|the consolidated case]].

### The CTC Tower

[[Willy Luditus]] constructed the [[CTC Tower]] directly behind Dominion Hall as a provocation against the imperial family. The structure cast a shadow over the palace and was declared a matter of national security. [[Porter Ehrveil]], then Secretary of Defense, demolished it with ceremonial cannons.

### The Imperial Wedding

Empress [[Kara A. Ehrveil]] and [[Riven Skorne]] were married in Dominion Hall, with the accompanying [[The Idesian Accord]] signed on the same occasion. The anniversary of the Accord is observed annually in New Terranova as the **Day of Reunion**.

### Ratification of the Provincial Constitution

[[Kara A. Ehrveil]] and [[Nordvik Alvaldi]] drafted [[The Constitution of the United Provinces of Lilaris]] together at Dominion Hall. Upon ratification by national referendum, New Terranova was formally designated the federal district of the Republic and its directly administered capital.
