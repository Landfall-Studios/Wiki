Founded by [[Edward Redcliffe]], Old Yotem is a meticulously guarded duchy that remains closed off to the public. More than merely a noble state, it functions primarily as a research outpost for the [[Ehrengard Empire]]. This dual role highlights Edward's forward-thinking strategy: melding traditional aristocratic influence with modern innovation to serve both his domains and the broader interests of the Empire.
