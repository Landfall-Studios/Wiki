The Red Diamond Casino is the flagship venue of the [[Red Corporation]], established by [[Edward Redcliffe]] as an exclusive destination that rapidly evolved into a premier site for impromptu get-togethers and high-stakes gambling. Beyond its reputation as a hub for entertainment, the casino served as Edward’s private residence for most of his time in [[New Terranova]], reflecting his penchant for merging luxury with personal sanctuary.

A notable feature of the casino is its state-of-the-art slot machines, developed and supplied by [[DaemonWare Labs]]. With their parent company, [[Citadel Corporation]], conveniently located right next door, the casino benefits from cutting-edge technology and seamless support.

Ownership of the Casino passed to [[Vetis Veltol]] as the first formal transfer in Edward's gradual handover of the Red Corporation, ahead of Edward's relocation to [[Old Yotem]]. Day-to-day operations of the venue were not visibly altered by the change in proprietor.
