## Overview

New Ides (pronounced _eye-dez_) is a nation nestled within [[The Frozen Mantle\|The Frozen Mantle]], renowned for its luxurious winemaking and cutting-edge technology sector. The duchy is home to [[Nouveaux Ides\|Nouveaux Ides]], the premier wine company of [[Locations/Caldora\|Caldora]], as well as Grape Inc., the developers of the innovative Seed OS.

Originally known as Ides, the region evolved into a duchy under [[Saludo\|Saludo]], taking on the name New Ides to mark its expansion. It later declared it's independence. Its founder, [[Isaac Acentino\|Isaac Acentino]], established [[Nouveaux Ides\|Nouveaux Ides]] as its first business, quickly elevating it to [[Caldora]]’s premier wine producer. Known for its aged vintages and refined selection, Nouveaux Ides has become a staple among collectors and connoisseurs across the land.

Beyond its economic prestige, New Ides is celebrated for its welcoming atmosphere and strong sense of community. The duchy is a hub of social gatherings, festivals, and cultural events, fostering a lively yet refined environment. As Nouveaux Ides Manor and the surrounding town continue to expand, New Ides is solidifying itself as both a center of luxury and innovation.
