## Overview

Derek Versalhes, Quality of Progression Affairs Leader of the [[Magda'roth Empire\|Magda'roth Empire]], is the youngest of the Versalhes twins and a pillar of strength and authority within the Empire. Driven by his father’s vision, Derek devotes himself to the protection of his brother, [[Desmond Versalhes\|Desmond Versalhes]], and the people of Magda'roth. His mastery of military strategy and technical expertise has positioned Magda'roth as a rising power, fortified against any external threat.

Derek commands absolute authority over the Guarda Vulpis, [[Magda'roth Empire\|Magda'roth Empire]]'s finest guard and military force to ensure order across the Empire’s territories. As both Imperial Forces Marshal and High Inquisitor, he wields the ultimate military and judicial power, acting as the last line of defense for Magda'roth. His stoic demeanor and unyielding approach to leadership have earned him respect and fear in equal measure.

## Appearance

- **Height**: 6'1"
- **Build**: Slim
- **Hair**: Pale blond
- **Eyes**: Orange, with a scar over his left eye
- **Clothing**: Derek wears a red and black mantle with military shoulder padding, paired with grey pants and black shoes. His head is adorned with a fox hood, the symbol of House Versalhes.

## Personality

Derek is stern, serious, and unflinchingly silent, choosing not to communicate verbally. His lack of speech and the absence of his left eye have fueled numerous rumors. Some believe he lost his eye and voice during a harrowing battle, where he single-handedly defeated an enemy squadron. Others whisper that his brother [[Desmond Versalhes\|Desmond Versalhes]] took them in exchange for granting him a leadership role. These rumors remain unverified, adding to Derek’s enigmatic and formidable reputation.

## Key Contributions

- **Military Leadership**: Derek’s strategic acumen ensures Magda'roth’s defenses are impenetrable, and his command over the military maintains order and stability.
- **Industrial Development**: His focus on fortifications and heavy industrialization drives Magda'roth’s transformation into an emergent powerhouse.

## Goals

Derek is dedicated to developing advanced equipment and fortifications to safeguard Magda'roth’s future, ensuring its people and territories are protected under his watchful leadership.
