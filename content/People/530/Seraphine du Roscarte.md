## Overview

Seraphine du Roscarte, the enigmatic Viscountess of [[Roscarte]] and sister of [[Étienne du Roscarte]] and [[Enzo du Roscarte]], is one of the most polarizing figures within the [[Ehrengard Empire]]. A woman of effortless charm and sharp wit, she blends the grace of aristocracy with the indulgence of high society. Officially the ruler of Roscarte, a secluded retreat for the empire’s elite, she paradoxically thrives in its extravagant social scene—whether for the wine, the endless gossip, or the loud music. Beneath her reputation as a carefree socialite, however, lies one of the Empire’s most valuable operatives, a woman whose influence extends far beyond the velvet curtains of Roscarte’s grand estates.

## Appearance

- **Hair**: Silver, often worn loose or in elegant disarray, reflecting her effortless, carefree demeanor.
- **Eyes**: Hazel
- **Build**: Slender and deceptively graceful; though often draped in noble attire, she moves with the precision of a practiced duelist.
- **Clothing**: A mix of extravagant court fashion and practical tailoring—deep jewel tones, fine silks, and layered outerwear that conveniently hides an assortment of weapons.

---

### Noble Lineage & Early Life

Born into the noble House du Roscarte, Seraphine was raised in an aristocratic family with old ties to the Empire, though her family’s real power waned generations ago. Roscarte itself remained a haven for the wealthy, a place where nobles could escape the pressures of court for indulgence, excess, and whispered conspiracies. From an early age, Seraphine understood the value of appearance—how playing the right part could get you further than brute force ever could.

### A Socialite with Secrets

While many believe she secured the Viscountess title purely through aristocratic connections, others suspect a deeper reason—one tied to her well-known yet unspoken role as an information broker and enforcer for the Empress. It is widely assumed that her revelry at Roscarte’s endless galas and social functions serves a secondary purpose: to gather intelligence, steer noble alliances, and ensure loyalty through carefully placed whispers—or, when necessary, a more permanent solution.

### The Empire’s Necessary Vice

Where Avery seeks to lead through unity and reform, Seraphine revels in the excesses of nobility while ensuring the Empire's survival through its darkest realities. While diplomacy is often preferred, she ensures compliance through the sheer weight of her influence within high society. If rumors arise of rebellion or treachery among the nobles, Seraphine is the first to hear them—often over an expensive glass of wine—and the last to let them fester.

---

## Philosophy

Seraphine does not delude herself with ideals of purity in governance. She understands that **no empire, no matter how noble in intent, will ever be universally loved**. Power demands sacrifice, and morality is often a luxury afforded only to those who don’t bear the weight of a kingdom. While Avery seeks to rule honorably, Seraphine ensures the Empire survives the realities of leadership.

Her philosophy can be summarized in her own words: **"If I must drown in wine and scandal to keep the Empire standing, then pour me another."**

Though she plays the role of detached indulgence well, those closest to her may notice the way she lingers in Avery’s presence just a little too long, how her sharpest words soften ever so slightly when directed at the Empress. Whether a lingering admiration or something deeper, Seraphine keeps such feelings locked behind laughter and an ever-full glass of wine.

---

### Reputation

- **Among Nobles:** The queen of Roscarte’s extravagant social scene—charming, sarcastic, and always seen with a drink in hand. A woman whose presence makes any gathering more interesting, but whose favor is as dangerous as her ire.
- **Among Military & Intelligence Circles:** A valuable but unconventional ally—her role may be unofficial, but her results are undeniable.
- **Among the People:** A noblewoman of no real consequence—just another aristocrat spending the Empire’s coin on fine wine and extravagant gowns. Exactly as she wants to be seen.

---

## Key Contributions

- **Ensuring Stability Through Social Intrigue:** Noble allegiances are as crucial as armies, and Seraphine controls them with masterful ease.
- **Intelligence & Counterespionage:** As a woman who is underestimated by nearly everyone, she gathers information where others cannot and ensures that those who betray the Empire do not live to regret it.

### Significance

Seraphine du Roscarte is a paradox—a noblewoman who thrives in the very system she mocks, a Viscountess whose rule is built on excess yet sustained by ruthless efficiency. While others concern themselves with honor, Seraphine simply raises a glass and ensures the Empire survives.
