## Overview

Desmond Versalhes, Political Affairs Leader of the [[Magda'roth Empire\|Magda'roth Empire]], is the eldest of the Versalhes twins and a notable figure in his nation’s diplomacy. Guided by his late father’s vision to rekindle the Magda'roth flame and elevate the Versalhes name, Desmond has tirelessly worked with his brother [[Derek Versalhes\|Derek Versalhes]] to position Magda'roth as a respected power in the world. His keen understanding of diplomacy and his unyielding dedication have earned him a reputation as both a capable negotiator and a man of the people.

Despite his noble status, Desmond is regarded by the citizens of Magda'roth as approachable and grounded. Often seen in public spaces, immersed in his work or lending a hand to townsfolk, he is admired for his humility and care-free attitude. This unassuming demeanor often leads outsiders to underestimate his political influence within the Empire.

Desmond’s primary focus lies in forging alliances with neighboring nations, seeking treaties and agreements that promise mutual growth and prosperity.

## Appearance

- **Height**: 5'5"
- **Build**: Slim
- **Hair**: Greyish-brown with a distinct white streak
- **Eyes**: Grey
- **Clothing**: Desmond wears a dark red and gold mantle with red shoulder padding, paired with black slim pants and boots. His head is adorned with a golden laurel crown, a subtle nod to his leadership role.

## Personality

Desmond is thoughtful but aloof, often lost in contemplation. While this introspective nature gives him insight into complex political matters, it also makes him prone to jumbled speech, a trait he is actively working to refine for the sake of more effective diplomacy. His sincerity and humility, however, endear him to both his people and potential allies.

## Key Contributions

- **Diplomatic Initiatives**: Desmond has been instrumental in establishing Magda'roth’s reputation on the global stage by pursuing alliances and fostering diplomatic relations with neighboring nations such as the [[Ehrengard Empire\|Ehrengard Empire]].
- **Civic Engagement**: His active involvement in public life has strengthened the bond between the Magda'roth leadership and its citizens, ensuring trust and cooperation within the Empire.

## Goals

Desmond seeks to further integrate the [[Magda'roth Empire\|Magda'roth Empire]] into the global political landscape, aiming to secure treaties that will bring new opportunities for his people. His vision for the Empire extends beyond its borders, fostering both economic growth and cultural exchange.
