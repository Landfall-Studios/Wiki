## Overview

Edward Redcliffe is a distinguished noble whose multifaceted roles span both aristocracy and entrepreneurial influence. As the Count of Everstead in [[New Terranova]] and the Duke of [[Old Yotem]], Edward commanded respect and authority in his domains. A devout follower of the [[Umbral Communion]], his spiritual beliefs underpin his decisions and alliances, while his ownership of the renowned [[Red Diamond Casino]] cements his reputation as a shrewd businessman.

## Background

Emerging from a legacy of both tradition and quiet defiance, Edward steadily ascended the ranks of power in [[New Terranova]] and the broader [[Ehrengard Empire]]. His deep commitment to the tenets of the [[Umbral Communion]] has guided his political and personal endeavors, ensuring that his influence resonates across both noble circles and common society. Throughout his career, Edward has balanced the demands of governance with the challenges of maintaining personal integrity, leaving his mark on the cultural and political landscapes of his territories.

## Holdings

Edward is the founder of [[Old Yotem]], a meticulously guarded duchy closed off to the public. The duchy functions primarily as a research outpost for the [[Ehrengard Empire]], reflecting his strategy of pairing traditional aristocratic influence with modern innovation in service of both his own domains and the broader interests of the Empire. He also retains his hereditary seat at Everstead within [[New Terranova]] and operates the [[Red Diamond Casino]] as a personal venture.

## The Veltol Brothers and the Red Corporation

The midpoint of [[Landfall-530 - Noble Blood]] brought the [[Vetis Veltol|Veltol brothers]] into [[New Terranova]] by way of the [[Ivory Reaches]]. Edward met them at the [[Red Diamond Casino]] and, against his usual habit of holding new acquaintances at a distance, offered both an immediate arrangement: a job on the Casino floor for the elder, [[Vetis Veltol|Vetis]], and a mentorship in trade for the younger, [[Veltic Veltol]]. Edward intended the arrangement to serve his own interests; the brothers were unaware of this and accepted in good faith.

What began as a calculated cultivation of two foreign newcomers became, over the following years, a genuine partnership with Vetis. The two became close friends, took to staging minor pranks on New Terranovan citizens together in their off hours, and eventually founded **Rat Co.** as a sub-brand of the [[Red Corporation]]. Ostensibly an overflow hospitality venture, Rat Co. served in practice as a vehicle for acquiring and operating private laboratories across Caldora. The first such site was a riverside property outside the city; by the end of Edward's life the network spanned numerous holdings, some of them sitting beneath occupied homes whose tenants never learned of the lower levels.

Edward kept up the suggestion, throughout the build-out, that the Rat Co. labs would make a fitting permanent home for the dragon egg Vetis had received from [[Nordvik Alvaldi]]. Vetis never agreed to it during Edward's lifetime.

## Old Yotem and Death

Late in the epoch the [[Ehrengard Empire]] pushed Edward to commit himself fully to the development of his duchy of [[Old Yotem]], and he relocated south accordingly. [[Veltic Veltol]] followed him rather than remain in [[New Terranova]]. From [[Old Yotem]] Edward handed the [[Red Corporation]] over to [[Vetis Veltol|Vetis]] in stages, until the whole of the holding was Vetis's outright.

Edward took his own life shortly after the transfer completed, in his quarters at the [[Red Diamond Casino]] on a return visit to [[New Terranova]]. He had been carrying an unspoken depression for several years; the asset transfers, read at the time as ordinary succession, were in hindsight the methodical closing of accounts of a man preparing to die. The events of his final week, including his last conversation with [[Avery R. Ehrveil]] on the patio of the Vesperine Temple and the hour and meaning of his death, are set out at length in [[The Death of Edward Redcliffe]], which also collects the signs his friends were unable to recognize in time.

## Personality and Quirks

Edward is known for his reserved and discerning nature. Not fond of new acquaintances, he grants trust sparingly, preferring to rely on those who have proven their loyalty over time. A signature element of his persona is the brass gauntlet he wears; a constant reminder of a defining moment from his past. Despite his cautious approach, Edward strives to display warmth and kindness, often breaking his stoic exterior with well-timed humor, thoughtful gifts, or congenial gestures. His pursuit of security, whether through formidable armor or the sanctuary of his estates, reflects an inner drive to maintain a stable and protected life amidst the uncertainties of power.
