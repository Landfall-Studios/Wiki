## Overview

Veltic Veltol is the younger brother of [[Vetis Veltol]] and an apprentice merchant under [[Edward Redcliffe]]. He arrived in [[New Terranova]] alongside his brother during the mid-period of [[Landfall-530 - Noble Blood]], following a long journey from their distant homeland, and has since spent the bulk of his career in Edward's mentorship rather than in his brother's circle.

---

## Appearance

Veltic shares the purple eyes and the physical features distinctive to the brothers' homeland, a region whose people resemble the populations of the distant deserts. He is the smaller and frailer of the two, and presents himself in royal attire complete with a crown that he is rarely seen without, in contrast to his brother's armored bearing.

---

## Background

### Arrival in New Terranova

Veltic was sixteen when the brothers entered [[New Terranova]] by way of the [[Ivory Reaches]]. The two were taken in by [[Edward Redcliffe]] at the [[Red Diamond Casino]], who placed [[Vetis Veltol|Vetis]] on the Casino payroll and offered Veltic an apprenticeship in trade under his own direct mentorship. Veltic had been pursuing a merchant's vocation since his earliest years and accepted the offer at once.

### Apprenticeship Under Edward

Veltic spent the apprenticeship years working out of the Casino's backrooms and learning the merchant side of the [[Red Corporation]] from Edward in person. The arrangement was the closest sustained mentor relationship he had ever held, and the bond between the two was strong enough that when the [[Ehrengard Empire]] later directed Edward to relocate and commit his time to the development of [[Old Yotem]], Veltic chose to follow him south rather than remain in [[New Terranova]] with his brother.

He has remained in [[Old Yotem]] since.
