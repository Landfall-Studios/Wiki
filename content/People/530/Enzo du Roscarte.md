#L530

#### **Overview**

Enzo du Roscarte is the hammer of House du Roscarte, the warrior who ensures that his family’s influence is not merely secured through words and wealth but through sheer, undeniable force. While [[Seraphine du Roscarte]] and [[Étienne du Roscarte]] maneuver through courts and ledgers, Enzo commands the unseen battlefield, ensuring that no threat to the Roscarte name is left standing.

A seasoned soldier, strategist, and enforcer, Enzo has no patience for noble games or the indulgences of courtly life. He operates in the shadows of power, wielding his skill as both a commander and a killer to eliminate obstacles before they ever reach his siblings. If Étienne is the calculating mind and Seraphine the cunning voice, then Enzo is the hand that strikes without hesitation.

---

### **Appearance**

- **Hair:** Silver, short and practical, often dusted with dirt or blood from battle
- **Eyes:** Hazel, cold and unflinching, revealing no emotion beyond ruthless efficiency
- **Build:** Broad-shouldered, powerful, with a body forged through years of war and bloodshed
- **Clothing:** Dark military-style attire, always functional, rarely adorned—save for a deep red cloak that drapes over his armor like a specter of violence

---

### **The Warrior of Roscarte**

Unlike his siblings, Enzo cares little for noble titles, estates, or high society. He does not waste time with pleasantries or manipulation. He understands that true power is not merely spoken or bought—it is taken, crushed into submission, and enforced with steel.

Where [[Étienne du Roscarte]] secures the Roscarte fortune and [[Seraphine du Roscarte]] ensures their influence, Enzo ensures their survival. He is the reason no rival dares challenge them openly.

- **Master Tactician:** He does not fight battles—he often ends them before they start. Every war, every skirmish, every duel is usually decided before the first blow is struck.
- **A Commander Without an Army:** Enzo commands no official force, yet he is never without soldiers. Whether mercenaries, private guards, or those who owe him their lives, he always has an army when he needs one.
- **The Enforcer of Roscarte:** Nobles who defy his family do not live to regret it. Deals are honored not by words, but by the unspoken threat that Enzo will ensure consequences.

---

### **The Shadow of the Battlefield**

Though born into nobility, Enzo is not a man of lavish estates or decadent feasts. He has spent more time on the battlefield than in the halls of [[Roscarte]], carving his reputation through war rather than diplomacy. His name is known in the underworld, among mercenaries and warlords, whispered in the dark by those who know what happens when the Roscarte family is crossed.

- **Feared by Nobles, Respected by Killers:** While court nobles see him as little more than a blunt instrument, warriors and assassins alike recognize him as something far worse—a mind honed for war, with the skill to execute it flawlessly.
- **The Butcher of Rebellions:** While others debate policies, Enzo simply ensures that threats are removed. Uprisings, rogue factions, and dissenters never gain the momentum to become real problems.
- **No Loyalty But Blood:** He does not serve kings or empires—he serves the Roscarte name, and nothing else.

---

### **Philosophy**

_"The strong rule. The weak serve. That is the only law that matters."_

Enzo does not believe in diplomacy, ideology, or sentimentality. He believes in strength—the ability to seize power and the will to keep it. He does not waste time with speeches or debates. If an obstacle exists, he removes it. If a threat rises, he crushes it.

Where [[Seraphine du Roscarte]] plays the long game and [[Étienne du Roscarte]] secures the foundations, Enzo ensures no one forgets that the Roscarte family is not to be challenged.

---

### **Key Contributions**

- **The Enforcer of Roscarte Power:** Ensuring that no rival dares to move against his siblings, either through intimidation or execution.
- **The Hidden Hand Behind Conflicts:** Wars and rebellions rarely begin without his knowledge—and most end before they ever begin.
- **Protector of the Roscarte Name:** His loyalty is to blood, and nothing else. So long as his siblings remain standing, his work continues.

---

### **Significance**

Enzo du Roscarte is the steel behind the Roscarte name, the warrior whose mere presence ensures their dominance remains unchallenged. While others seek power through wealth and influence, Enzo reminds the world that true power is carved in blood.
