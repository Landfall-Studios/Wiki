## Overview
`"Lo' the man that spits at others. None but the skies know why. . ."`
*— Unknown*

Previous aviator, Hermet lays low and out of sight. Alone, as he'd prefer. No one knows his history, yet it remains deep and ingrained in the work he does.

He's estimated to be around 50 years of age, and surely shows it in his voice and demeanor. Horribly stuck in his past and a recluse. A lean build and stands around five and half feet tall.

# Background
Little is known of the man's life before establishing the Northern Strip. With little clues hinted and left around his work, all that could be pieced together is the man has a knack for aviation. Clearly a pilot in a previous life. Hermet displays years of work experience in realm of those craft. Too, his hands work hard with any blue-collared skill. Weapon-smithing, maitenence, and light electrical and computer work.

In terms of family, there are no publicly known relatives; though the man holds a soft spot whenever placed into parental roles. Hinting at the possibility of children. Additionally, he holds a hard grudge against cybernetically enhanced beings. And a complete distaste for robotic creature outside of his own creation. To why? None know but him.

# The Northern Airstrip
In the year [RP date of Aug 2026], Hermet began settling down north of Fuhai in a small pennisula. Originally, just a workshop, now turned into 2 runways with multiple buildings around. In the center, a tower monitoring the airspace around.

The Strip remains neutral and a non-combative zone to this day. It does not deal with the politics around it.

# Theft and Raids
A month after his appearance, Hermet made a pilgrimage to an undisclosed location; disappearing for a week's time. In that time, the Strip was raided and looted by pirates. Scattering material across the shops and many of the fuel sources established were ripped from the walls and stolen. Upon his returnal, Hermet retracted any service and promise of utilities the Strip would provide. He, too, installed a mounted auto-cannon turret upon the top of his building (to which he removed later in the week due to accidental shell-breaking.)

Since so, Hermet has verbally threated multiple passerbys to remain a good distance away from the strip. Visitors are often met with empty-threats and grumbles from the man.

# The GSS
While entirely recluse and anti-social, Hermet has been spotted occasionally speaking with GSS officials. Their visits are brief, and occasionally, parcels are exchanged. Asked on the matter, Hermet denies any involvment in GSS dealings.
