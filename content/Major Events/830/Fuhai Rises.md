On November 3rd, 2026, one side of Fuhai's mountain was utterly demolished in an attack against a GSS member's home. A massive crater now stands where the house once stood. Shortly after, a statement was released from the Office of the President telling members of Fuhai that a meeting and press conference will be held that evening in the newly renovated government building. What follows is a recounting of that meeting.

--- 
# The Speech
The initial phase of the meeting was a speech by Marsh Horton. In attendance were members of Fuhai, Jane Harbinger, members of the GSS, and a reporter from Citadel. Marsh's speech had three parts:
## The Attack
Marsh described the attack, what is currently known, and what the government is planning on doing about it. Fuhai is launching an investigation into the perpetrator, as well as the whereabouts of the homeowner. Marsh expressed her concern that Citadel may have been behind the attack.
## The Defend Democracy Initiative
Marsh described the country's new commitment to security with regard to the new Defend Democracy Initiative, a multi-part plan for strengthening Fuhai against attacks like these. Fuhai plans to place automatic missiles in strategic positions around the city to defend from air attacks. Fuhai will also install player detection systems in the area.
## We Rise
Marsh finally unveiled the government's final plan for security: off-world relocation. The Fuhai government is planning on building an orbital shuttle around Caldora as a safe haven and living space for citizens concerned about attacks from Citadel. The plan will go into effect in a few years, as the ship has not been fully developed yet. 

---
# The Questioning
After the initial speech, the crowd began to ask Marsh questions, some of which are listed here:
1. Members of the GSS asked why Fuhai is "running away" instead of waging war on Citadel to free those still trapped. Marsh responded that the citizens of Fuhai are her first priority, and that they do not have the resources to fight the corporate gods in the Citadel.
2. A reporter from Citadel accused Marsh of ignoring the existence of other democratic nations in the area and abandoning them. Marsh responded that Fuhai will not be severing ties with its own cities and other nations, and instead, will continue operating on Caldora, just to a lesser extent. Fuhai citizens will be able to go to and from the shuttle on a regular schedule if desired.

Despite Marsh's best efforts, the tone of the meeting was very negative after the many questions raised. 

---
# The Aftermath
After the conference, posters began to pop up around Fuhai showing President Marsh in space with the text "We Rise" above and below. Some have reported that this language and presentation makes Marsh seem much more authoritarian than she has been in the past. As well, a continuous squabble between her and the leadership in TUNA came to a head as Sara was removed as Administrator over TUNA, being replaced by Jane Harbinger. Jane has made many improvements to TUNA, preparing it for it's new job: a place to stay for those prepping for spaceflight in the coming years. Sara has asked for TUNA to be released back as an independent nation, but has been so far rejected by Marsh.
